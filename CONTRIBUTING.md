# Contributing

Issues and discussion are welcome.

Pull requests that change normative text in SPEC.md require prior written agreement with Craig Thomas Ellrod as maintainer. Craig Thomas Ellrod is responsible for the editorial text of the specification; conformance is determined from the records (SPEC Section 22).

Submitting a contribution does not create a patent grant from you, or from Craig Thomas Ellrod. Patent rights are reserved by Craig Thomas Ellrod. All contributors and implementers must follow the attribution requirements in NOTICE.md.

- Declare AI versus human contribution in AI_DISCLOSURE.md and ai_disclosure.json.
